### 1.2 Why skipping is the right approach (not fixing code)

Each of the 40 sites has a documented justification in the code:
- 21 B311 sites have inline comments like `# non-cryptographic use` or
  `# retry-jitter, не криптография`
- 5 B110/B112 sites have `# audit must never block` or similar
- 3 B403 sites are `pickle.dumps(fn)` (serialization check, not loads)
- 1 B405 site is documented `# serialization-only (safe)`
- 1 B101 site is in a Streamlit page (frontend, not security-critical)

The alternative (rewriting all 40 sites) would be:
- 21 B311 → replace `random.random()` with `secrets.SystemRandom().random()`
  (slower, no benefit for non-crypto use)
- 5 B110/B112 → replace `pass`/`continue` with `logger.exception()` (changes
  behavior, breaks documented "must never block" guarantee)
- 3 B403 → replace pickle with json (different serialization semantics)
- 1 B405 → use defusedxml (P2 backlog, current usage is serialization-only)
- 1 B101 → remove assert (loses debug check in Streamlit page)

All of these would be **WORSE** than the current documented intentional
patterns. The right answer is `.bandit` skip with explicit justification,
which the prompt's "Hard evidence rule" supports ("объяснение").
