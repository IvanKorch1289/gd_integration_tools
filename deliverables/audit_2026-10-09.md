   developer inner-loop until resolved.

   **0 actual instances of buggy single-name form** were detected in
   this codebase; all 100+ occurrences are the tuple form which still
   works as intended. **Status: REJECTED AS STALE** for the "Python 2
   syntax" framing — there's no SyntaxError to fix.
